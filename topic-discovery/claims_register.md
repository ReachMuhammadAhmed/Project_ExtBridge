# Claims register

**Purpose:** a running list of every factual claim the paper will make, each with its source, its evidence tag, and whether it has been verified. Nothing goes into the paper unless it is in this register and verified. Every claim behind a gap statement must be checked against its source; only claims with `supports = yes` may be used. Update it in Stage 5B and Stage 8 (see `methodology.md`).
